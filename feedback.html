Help keep the
guide useful.

Found something that needs fixing? Have an experience that could help the next class? Send it here. A student maintainer reviews every submission before anything is added to the guide.

Who can submit Anyone can report an error. Student experiences, group information and mentor profiles should come from current or recent UofT MD students.
A source helps For dates, requirements, fees or policies, include the official page or document when you can. It makes the correction easier to verify.
The Process

What happens after you submit

Submissions do not appear automatically. Information is checked for accuracy, relevance and permission before it is considered for the guide.

1

You submit

Choose the closest option below and include enough detail to locate or understand the issue. Your email is optional unless follow-up is needed.

2

A maintainer reviews

A current student maintainer checks the submission. Factual changes are compared with an official or otherwise clearly identified source.

3

The guide is updated

Approved material is added to the relevant page. Housing reviews remain student-reported. Community information is published only when the submitter is authorized to share it.

Submit

What would you like to contribute?

Choose the closest option. The questions below will change with your selection.

i
Keep private information out of the form

Do not submit patient information, passwords, financial documents, confidential course materials or identifying information about someone else without their permission. Contact details entered as “public” may appear on the site.

Submit a housing review

Reviews appear with the relevant building in the Housing section. Details about commute, management, noise and what you wish you knew are especially useful.

Will appear on the public review. Most reviewers use "[year] student" e.g., "2T7 student".

Out of 5.

What worked well? What did not? What would you tell your past self before signing? Two to four sentences is a useful starting point.

Include the year, unit type and whether utilities were included. This may be published as part of your review, so leave it blank if you would rather not share it.

Only used to notify you when your review is approved. Never published.

By submitting, you confirm this review reflects your own experience as a UofT MD student.

Community group content

Add or update information for a community group in the Field Guide. Please submit only on behalf of a group you represent.

For example, co-president, member or past executive. This is used for verification and is not published.

What does the group do? How can incoming students connect? What programs or supports should they know about?

A group email, website or social account. This will be displayed publicly.

For follow-up if we have questions. Never published.

By submitting, you confirm you have the group's permission to provide this content.

Spotted something wrong?

Tell us what is out of date, factually incorrect or unclear. Specific details and a source help us check the issue properly.

Include an official webpage, document or clearly identified student source when possible. If the source is not public, describe where it can be found.

The change will be reviewed against the source before the page is updated.

General feedback

Use this for a suggestion, request or comment that does not fit the other options.

Only if you want a response.

Include an email if you would like a reply or if the maintainer may need more detail.

Sign up as a mentor

Your approved profile will appear in the mentor directory with your name, short introduction and chosen contact method.

For example, mature student, commuter life, LGBTQ+, finances, accommodations or international student.

In your own voice. What you wish you'd known, what you're willing to talk about, why you signed up.

Email, Instagram, or however you prefer to be contacted. This will be public on your card.

Share a link only if you have permission for the photo to be cropped and displayed publicly on your mentor profile.

Used for follow-up only. If this is also your chosen public contact, it will appear because you entered it above.

By submitting, you agree to the public display of your profile and chosen contact method. You can request an update or removal at any time.

Share a testimonial

Short reflections that help incoming students. These appear on the relevant page (housing, academics, etc.).

How you'll be credited. Most use "[year] student."

Two to four sentences is a useful starting point. Specific details, such as a door-to-door commute time or a study approach that changed, are most helpful.

Student perspectives are labelled as student-reported and may be lightly edited for length or clarity.

Update or remove a listing

Use this for a mentor profile, community listing, testimonial or other information connected to you or a group you represent.

Name the page and section. Include a link if you have one.

This helps confirm that you can request the change.

Used to verify the request and confirm any needed details. It will not be published.

Only share the information needed to locate the listing and verify your request.
